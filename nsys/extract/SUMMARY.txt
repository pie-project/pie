cutlass gemm_relu 256x128 or the ampere 128x128 64x3 gemm. Gap = first kernel of step N+1 minus last kernel end of step N.
Host side: in every large gap the next step's cudaGraphLaunch starts late (launch call start minus step N end is
nearly the whole gap; the call itself takes 0.4-3.3 ms; the device starts within about 2 us of the call returning).
Ordinary large gaps (3-50 ms): the launching thread makes only 9 CUDA calls (about 1 ms in all): it is not waiting
on the device in a CUDA call; it is busy or blocked outside CUDA (no OS-runtime trace was captured to tell which).
Boundary gaps (shared: 2 per window, 170-228 ms; private: 1, 79 ms): each precedes a prefill-carrying step, with
5.7k (private) to 14k (shared) CUDA calls on the thread (cudaMemcpyAsync, cudaMemsetAsync, cudaEventQuery/Record/
CreateWithFlags), 11-39 ms of call time; the rest of the gap is outside CUDA calls as well.
