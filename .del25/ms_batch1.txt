Every stated symbol is a KERNEL (170), an OP (10) or a SERVICE (14).
`Execution` in `src/execution.rs` is that distinction as data; `kind_of_wall`
is the mapping, and `assert_total` derives the service set twice -- from the
walls here and from `execution::SERVED` -- and asserts the two agree.

  A SERVICE is never work: cuBLAS, CUTLASS, NCCL, the P2P all-reduce plane, or
  the driver itself. It cannot migrate because it was never a kernel of ours.
  An OP is a host program over kernels of ours, most of which migrate already;
  what a row cannot say is the composition -- and `Step` now says some of it.
  So the third line is the migration's real denominator, and the second is the
  one that shows how much of the shortfall is not work at all.
