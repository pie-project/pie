PLANNED is what tart actually emits: merged on the axes where merging wins and
padded on the axis where plan.py returns "padded". It is the only column that is
never worse than both of the pure strategies, and the pure MERGED column is the
refutation of the obvious hypothesis -- composing axes does not compound the
mechanism's advantage, it compounds whichever axis the mechanism loses.

Read down the 'vs padded' column within one row count. Each axis on its own is
something padding can absorb; the product is what it cannot, because the pad
factors multiply while the merged pass is doing the same thing it was already
doing. 'pad waste' is that product computed from the shapes alone -- the extra
arithmetic padding signs up for, before any measurement.

This is the argument for a general mechanism stated as a measurement. A point
solution per axis leaves you with the product, and the product is the case
vLLM's own table calls mutually exclusive.
